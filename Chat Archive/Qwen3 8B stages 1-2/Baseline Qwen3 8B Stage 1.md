  - **Start small** by saying hello or asking a question (e.g., “Do you

### **3. Practice Being Yourself**
  - Authenticity is key. People are drawn to genuine, kind, and curious

person this week) and celebrate progress.

### **4. Build Trust and Relationships**
  - **Show interest in others** by asking about their lives, hobbies, or

  - Friendships take time to develop. Don’t rush the process.

### **6. Take Care of Your Well-Being**

can help you navigate emotions and build skills for connection.
